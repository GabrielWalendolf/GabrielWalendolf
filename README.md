## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/hvRJCLFzcasrR4ia7z/giphy.gif" width="28" /> About me

I'm a **Full Stack Developer** and **Software Engineering student at Univille** (Joinville, SC, Brazil). I design, build and ship complete products: from the database schema and API to the interface and the deploy.

Most of my work today is **software with social impact**. I develop and maintain the systems used by **Universo Down**, a nonprofit that offers free therapies to people with Down syndrome, and I take on freelance projects for small businesses.
